# Mizu design maturity audit

The audit began at `b975e09`, directly after the 0.3.0 release, and reviewed the complete catalog of 79 components, nine blocks, the landing page, the documentation shell, the brand assets, and five open dependency pull requests. It asked one question: would a design team at a product company choose Mizu to carry their AI product, their app, and their website, and be proud to show the result?

The work shipped in stages: 0.3.1 for dependencies, 0.4.0-rc.1 for motion, 0.4.0-rc.2 for components and blocks, then the site and brand, and finally 0.4.0. An independent review of the complete change before release found 25 defects; each is fixed in 0.4.0 and listed in its changelog entry.

## Visual direction

References were [details.so](https://www.details.so/) for editorial restraint and a serif accent, [bencho.dev](https://bencho.dev/) for a gallery of live, cursor-driven components on soft gray cards, [SpaceUI's squishmoji](https://www.spaceui.one/tools/avatars?view=gallery&type=squishmoji) for a soft, expressive character, and Grok for a calm composer and a rotating headline word.

The palette is unchanged: black, white, and neutral grays carry the interface, and the water gradient marks the brand and AI presence. Depth still comes from tonal fills and soft shadows. The site pairs Inter with a single italic serif word per headline.

Motion follows four principles, documented at `/docs/motion`: respond at once, settle softly, feel physical when touched, and stay quiet at rest.

## Verification

Every stage passed formatting, linting, type checks, contract and unit tests, registry validation, an isolated consumer install of every registry item, a production build within the bundle budget, and the Playwright suite. The suite runs axe on every component page and block in light and dark themes at desktop and 320 pixel widths, with reduced motion.

New components carry behavior contracts in their tests: stable text for assistive technology beneath visual effects, announcements for copy and hold confirmation, radio semantics for SegmentedControl, `aria-current` and announced badges for TabBar, and a pause mechanism for continuous motion under WCAG 2.2.2.

Representative manual VoiceOver review, which ADR 0003 requires before a stable minor release, was still outstanding at release; the 0.4.0 changelog entry records the exception. Automated checks are evidence for the rules they test, not a substitute for that review.
